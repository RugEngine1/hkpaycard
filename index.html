⚠️ Important: Card Usage Policy

Cards may be frozen or canceled (with or without balance refund) under the following risk conditions. Please use your card responsibly.

  • Failed transactions exceed 25% of monthly total
  • High number of consecutive failed transactions
  • High number of consecutive zero-amount transactions
  • Failed transaction significantly above card balance
  • Use at high-risk merchants (gambling, gas stations, financial services)
  • Excessive refund transactions (AML compliance)
  • Card unused for more than 30 days
